Analytical Framework

Methodology & AI Modeling Pipeline

End-to-end integration of petabyte-scale satellite constellations, bioclimatic reanalysis, spatio-temporal deep learning, and explainable AI.

Stage 01

Multi-Source EO Ingestion

Cloud-masked Sentinel-2 MSI (10m) & Landsat 5/7/8/9 surface reflectance cubes, harmonized with ERA5-Land 0.1° gridded climate reanalysis.

Stage 02

Feature Engineering

Computation of photosynthetic & canopy water indices (NDVI, EVI, SAVI, NDMI), thermal stress (LST), and multi-scalar drought indices (SPEI-1/3/6).

Stage 03

Geo-AI Ensemble Models

Spatial Random Forest, Gradient Boosted Trees (LightGBM/XGBoost), and Spatio-Temporal ConvLSTM neural networks tuned for yield anomaly detection.

Stage 04

Risk Synthesis & XAI

Relative Hazard Index (RHI) calculation, SHAP feature importance interpretation, and CMIP6 scenario projection up to 2050 for CAP policy support.

Academic Disclaimer & Open License

The datasets, machine learning models, and risk forecasts published on this portal are generated strictly for academic and scientific research within the PhD project GEO-AIYLRF at Charles University.

While models are calibrated using empirical ground-truth harvest statistics and Copernicus satellite data, future projections reflect CMIP6 climate model assumptions and should not be used as the sole basis for commercial grain market speculation or definitive insurance claims.

Data and source code are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY 4.0) and MIT licenses.
